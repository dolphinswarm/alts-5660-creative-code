Week 3: Clock

A "marble clock" built with Pixi.js, Matter.js, and Tone.js. Four chutes drop marbles on their own real-seconds interval (1s / 2s / 4s / 8s), with each marble/peg collision playing a random note from a chord. The chord changes every 15 seconds, and each peg has a small chance of playing a note outside the current chord.

Tap the board to start the sound. Click a peg to "remove"/"re-add" it.
