🐿️ Week 6: Data Visualization 🌰

A Three.JS visualization of the 2018 Central Park Squirrel Census: 3,023 squirrel sightings across 350 hectares of Central Park over 11 days in October 2018. The 2,919 shown here are the ones with a recorded fur color and location.

Drag to move around the park, scroll to zoom, and right-drag (or Shift-drag) to rotate. Click a squirrel to fly down to it and see what the census recorded about it. Every squirrel is circled in its fur color, and squirrels hidden under the trees show through as faint silhouettes. The filters below narrow down which ones are shown.
