🎸 Week 2: Poster Generator 🤘

A generative DIY show-flyer poster, built with PixiJS. Every band name, venue, date, color, and shape is randomly generated from a fixed set of building blocks.

Click 🎲 for a brand new poster. Click 🎵 to keep the poster and just swap the song. Click 🎨 to keep the same layout and just swap the color palette.

Click the poster itself to play some Tone.js music! The stickers are audio-reactive and will animate. Clicking on it again will stop it.
